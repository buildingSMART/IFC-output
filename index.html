buildingSMART International Standards Server

This is the official domain for buildingSMART International standards and supporting documentation.

This includes Retired, Official, and Candidate versions of the IFC schemas, as well as MVD documentation (HTML), along with other associated files (EXP, XSD, RDF, TTL, etc.). Support documentation in PDF form may also be found here.

All content on this server can be accessed from links at the following buildingSMART websites:

  • buildingsmart.org
  • technical.buildingsmart.org
  • forums.buildingsmart.org

If there are any questions or issues, please contact websupport@buildingsmart.org or technical@buildingsmart.org.

Thank you and welcome to the buildingSMART International community!
